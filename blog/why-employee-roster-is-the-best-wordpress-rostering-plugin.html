Stop Paying Per Seat: Why Employee Roster Is the Best WordPress Staff Rostering Plugin

2 May 2026 WordPress Plugin

If you're running a small business on WordPress and paying $4–$8 per employee per month for a SaaS rostering tool like Deputy or When I Work, you're probably spending $500–$2,000 a year just to schedule your staff.

There's a better way.

Employee Roster is a WordPress shift scheduling plugin that replaces your SaaS rostering tool entirely — and lives right inside your WordPress admin. One annual licence. No per-seat fees. Your data stays on your own server.

Here's why it's the smartest choice for any WordPress-powered business.

1. It Lives Inside WordPress — Where You Already Work

Most employee scheduling tools are standalone SaaS platforms. That means another login, another tab, another subscription, and another third-party system holding your staff data.

Employee Roster is a native WordPress plugin. Your managers schedule shifts from the same WordPress admin they use every day. Your employees clock in, request leave, and check their roster from a frontend portal built directly into your existing site — no separate app required.

If your business already runs on WordPress, adding a rostering tool that works inside it is simply the smarter choice.

2. No Per-User Fees — Ever

This is the biggest financial argument for Employee Roster.

Competing SaaS tools charge per employee per month. At 20 staff members, Deputy costs roughly $90–$120/month. When I Work runs a similar model. Those costs compound every year, for every new hire.

Employee Roster's Starter plan is $99/year flat — regardless of how many shifts you schedule or how many employees clock in. The Professional plan is $199/year and unlocks every feature including leave balance tracking, tax calculations, daily budget tracking, and shift swap requests.

For a business with 20 staff, that's potentially $1,000+ in annual savings compared to a per-seat SaaS tool.

3. Complete Shift Scheduling With Everything You Need

Employee Roster isn't a stripped-down scheduling widget. It's a full workforce management system built for real businesses:

  • Shift scheduling — Create shifts with direct assignment or open slots that employees self-assign. View your team in a weekly roster or monthly calendar. Copy an existing week's roster to save time rebuilding the same schedule.
  • Clock in / clock out — Staff clock in from the WordPress dashboard or the frontend portal. Break tracking and shift linking are included. One active session per user is enforced so records stay clean.
  • Leave management — Employees submit annual or sick leave requests in seconds. Managers approve or decline with one click. Professional plan users get full leave balance tracking with per-job-type entitlements.
  • Timesheets and payroll — Weekly summaries per employee with total hours, pay rate calculations, optional tax, and daily labour budget tracking against a configurable target. Export to CSV for your accountant or payroll software.
  • Shift swap requests — Employees request swaps between each other. Managers review and approve — shift assignments update automatically.
  • Staff availability — Each employee sets their weekly recurring availability. Availability hints appear inside the shift assignment modal so managers spot conflicts instantly without leaving the screen.

4. Your Data Stays on Your Server

This is a point most SaaS vendors gloss over.

When you use a cloud rostering tool, your staff names, pay rates, attendance records, and leave history all live on someone else's server — subject to their privacy policy, their data retention practices, and their uptime.

With Employee Roster, everything is stored in your own WordPress database. There are no external APIs, no SaaS dependencies, and no third-party servers involved in day-to-day operation. If you cancel your licence, your historical data doesn't disappear — it stays in your database forever.

For businesses in industries with strict data handling requirements — healthcare, childcare, finance — this distinction matters enormously.

5. Role-Based Access Built for Real Teams

Employee Roster ships with three purpose-built roles:

  • Roster Manager — full scheduling and reporting access
  • Roster Supervisor — manage shifts and staff within their scope
  • Roster Employee — view own shifts, clock in/out, submit leave

Managers see only the Employee Roster menu when logged into WordPress. Employees never see plugin names, settings, or unrelated admin pages. Profile Mode lets you block employee WordPress logins entirely while email notifications keep working — useful for businesses that want employees to receive schedule updates by email without any WordPress access.

6. A Frontend Portal Your Staff Will Actually Use

Not every employee wants to log into WordPress. Employee Roster automatically creates five shortcode-powered frontend pages when the plugin activates:

  • Dashboard
  • My Shifts
  • Leave requests
  • Clock In / Clock Out
  • Availability settings

Employees access these pages from your existing website — no app download, no separate login system, no extra subscription. The same polished interface works on desktop and mobile.

7. Free Plan to Get Started, Paid Plans That Scale

Employee Roster has a genuinely useful free tier — not a crippled demo. Free plan users get up to 5 employees, 15 active shifts, day and weekly roster views, and full clock-in/out functionality.

When your team grows, Starter ($99/year) unlocks up to 25 employees, monthly calendar view, open shifts, leave approvals, payroll calculations, email notifications, and CSV export. Professional ($199/year) adds unlimited staff, leave balance tracking, tax calculations, daily budget targets, shift swaps, and PDF roster export. Business ($599/year) covers 5 WordPress sites — ideal for franchises, agencies, or multi-location groups.

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

8. Built for WordPress — No Compromises

Employee Roster is engineered specifically for WordPress, not bolted on top of it:

  • Uses wp_ajax handlers — immune to REST API restrictions from other plugins
  • Sends email via wp_mail() — works with any WordPress mail setup
  • Stores data in your own wp_ database tables — no external database required
  • Respects WordPress roles and capabilities throughout
  • Compatible with WordPress 5.8+, PHP 7.4+, MySQL 5.7+

There's no monthly cloud sync, no webhook dependency, and no "connection lost" errors when a third-party API goes down.

Who Is Employee Roster For?

Employee Roster is the right fit if you:

  • Already run your business website on WordPress
  • Have a small-to-medium team (5–100 staff)
  • Are tired of paying per-seat SaaS fees for rostering
  • Want your staff scheduling data on your own server
  • Need shift scheduling, attendance tracking, leave management, and payroll in one place

It's particularly well-suited for cafes and restaurants, retail stores, healthcare and childcare providers, cleaning and trades businesses, and any small business using WordPress that needs a professional rostering solution without enterprise pricing.

If your business runs on WordPress, there is no better employee scheduling plugin than Employee Roster. It gives you everything a standalone SaaS rostering tool offers — shift scheduling, attendance tracking, leave management, payroll calculations, email notifications, and a frontend employee portal — at a fraction of the annual cost, with your data staying exactly where it belongs: on your own server.
